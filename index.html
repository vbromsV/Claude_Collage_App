    Gestures

    • Tap a slot or text to select it.
    • One finger drag moves the selected image or text.
    • Pinch zooms the image (0.5×–5×). On text, pinch changes the size.
    • Two-finger twist rotates the image (can be turned off below).
    • Drag a divider line to resize the slots.
    • Double-tap text to edit it.
    • Tap an empty slot to pick an image for it.
    • Shuffle buttons in the top bar randomize a slot (L/M/R or T/M/B) or all slots from their collections, without opening the menu.

    GIFs

    • In the editor a GIF shows its first frame as a still picture.
    • If the collage contains a GIF, it is exported as an animated GIF. Otherwise it is exported as JPEG.
    • The first GIF in the collage (left/top first) sets the animation: its frames and timing are used.
    • Other images, including any other GIFs, appear as still pictures in the exported GIF.
    • GIFs are exported at 720 px on the long side and up to 256 colors per frame, so they stay a reasonable size. JPEGs are exported at 2048 px.

    Settings

    Privacy

    In the Android app, saved collages go to Pictures/Collage and show up in Google Photos.

    Everything happens on your device. Your images are never uploaded anywhere. The app works offline once it has been opened.
